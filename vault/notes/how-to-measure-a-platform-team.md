# How to measure a platform team

Platform teams struggle to show their value because their best work is invisible: things simply stop breaking and start being easy.

I measure platform success with adoption and time, not output. How many teams use the platform when they could choose not to? How long does it take a new service to go from idea to production? How long does a new engineer take to ship their first change?

When we started tracking time to first production deploy for new services, it was 11 days. After a year of platform work it was under 2. That single number told leadership more than any list of features we'd built.

Pick two or three numbers that your users would care about, measure them honestly, and share them every month. If they don't move, change what you're building.
