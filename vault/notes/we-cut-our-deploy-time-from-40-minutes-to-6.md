# We cut our deploy time from 40 minutes to 6.

We cut our deploy time from 40 minutes to 6. The biggest win wasn't caching or parallel tests. It was deleting 300 tests that had not failed in two years and tested nothing anyone cared about. Every test is a promise you keep paying for. Audit your promises.
