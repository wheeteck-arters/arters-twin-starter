# My first 90 days as an engineering lead

When I stepped up to lead a team for the first time, I made the classic mistake: I kept coding as much as before and treated leadership as extra work on top.

What finally worked was a 90-day plan in three parts. In the first 30 days I listened: one-to-ones with every engineer, every stakeholder, and the support team who hear customer pain first. In the next 30 days I fixed one visible thing the team had complained about for months, which for us was a flaky test suite. That bought trust. In the last 30 days I set out a simple direction for the next two quarters and wrote it down.

The lesson I'd pass on: your job is no longer to be the best engineer in the room. It's to make the room work. Coding becomes something you do when it unblocks others, not when it's the most fun task available.
