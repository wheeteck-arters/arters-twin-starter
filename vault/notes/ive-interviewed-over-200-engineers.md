# I've interviewed over 200 engineers.

I've interviewed over 200 engineers. The signal I trust most isn't the algorithm question. It's how someone talks about a bug they caused. People who can describe their own mistake calmly, with what they learned and what they changed, tend to be the ones who make teams safer.
