# The best on-call rotation I ever ran had a rule: if you get paged...

The best on-call rotation I ever ran had a rule: if you get paged twice for the same thing, fixing it becomes the top priority for the next sprint. Not a ticket in the backlog. The top priority. Pages are a tax your past self charges your future self, and someone has to stop paying it.
