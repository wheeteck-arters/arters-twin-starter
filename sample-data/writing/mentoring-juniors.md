---
title: What I tell junior engineers
date: 2026-06-30
---

Every few months a junior engineer asks me what they should focus on. My answer has barely changed in ten years.

Read more code than you write. Pick a well-run open-source project and read how they structure things, name things and review things.

Ask questions early. A question after 30 minutes of being stuck is a sign of good judgement, not weakness. A question after three days is a missed deadline.

Learn to debug methodically: reproduce, isolate, form one hypothesis at a time, and write down what you've ruled out.

And write down what you learn. A simple notes file of problems and solutions becomes, after a year, the most useful document you own. It's also, I've discovered, the start of a personal knowledge base that AI tools can work with.
