---
title: Why engineering teams should write more
date: 2025-12-15
---

The highest-leverage habit in a distributed team is writing. Not more documentation for its own sake, but writing as a way to think and decide.

When a decision lives only in a meeting, it lives only in the memories of the people who attended, and memories drift. When it lives in a short written note, new joiners can find it, people in other time zones can comment on it, and future you can understand why past you chose it.

I ask for three kinds of writing on my teams: design docs for anything that takes more than two weeks, decision records for choices we might regret, and short weekly updates that say what shipped, what's blocked and what's next.

The pushback is always time. But a one-page doc that takes an hour to write saves several hours of meetings and weeks of rework. Writing is not overhead. It's how a team remembers.
