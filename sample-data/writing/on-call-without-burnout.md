---
title: On-call without burnout
date: 2025-11-20
---

On-call is where engineering culture shows its real face. A team can talk about wellbeing all day, but if the same three people carry the pager every holiday, everyone knows what the team actually values.

My rules for a healthy rotation are simple. Every engineer who ships to production joins the rotation, including leads. Shifts are one week, with a proper handover note. Anyone paged at night starts late the next day, no questions asked. And every page gets a short review on Monday: was it real, was it actionable, and could a machine have handled it?

The Monday review is what changes things. Alerts that aren't actionable get deleted or downgraded. Repeat pages become sprint work. Within two quarters, our out-of-hours pages fell from around 30 a month to fewer than 8.

On-call should be boring. If it's exciting, your system is telling you something.
