---
title: Treat technical debt like a budget
date: 2026-02-25
---

Arguing about technical debt in every planning meeting wastes everyone's energy. On my teams we agreed a budget instead: about 20 percent of each sprint goes to reducing debt, and the engineers decide how to spend it.

The budget works because it removes the fight. Product managers know the time is reserved, so they plan around it. Engineers know they don't need permission for each fix, so they pick the debt that hurts most today.

We keep a short list of debt items ranked by pain: how often it slows us down, how often it causes incidents, and how scary it is to change. Every quarter we look back at what the budget bought us. Usually it's faster builds, fewer pages and happier new joiners, which is an easy story to tell leadership.

Debt isn't bad. Unmanaged debt is.
