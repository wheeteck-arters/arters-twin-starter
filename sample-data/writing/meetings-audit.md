---
title: Audit your meetings every quarter
date: 2026-09-02
---

Meetings multiply quietly. Each one made sense when it was created, and nobody ever deletes them.

Every quarter I audit my team's recurring meetings with three questions. What decision or outcome did this meeting produce in the last month? Could it be a written update instead? Who attends but never speaks?

Last quarter the audit removed four recurring meetings and shortened two more. That gave every engineer back about three hours a week, which is nearly two working weeks a quarter per person.

The meetings that survive are the ones where people need to think together: design reviews, retrospectives and one-to-ones. Status updates moved to writing, where they're faster to read and easier to search.
