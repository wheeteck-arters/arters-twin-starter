---
title: Psychological safety starts in the incident channel
date: 2026-10-20
---

You can tell how safe a team feels by reading its incident channel during an outage.

In unsafe teams, people hesitate. They wait to be sure before saying anything, because being wrong in public feels costly. Meanwhile the outage continues.

In safe teams, people say what they see as soon as they see it, including 'I just deployed something, could it be me?' That one sentence can cut an outage from an hour to five minutes.

Leaders create this safety by how they react in the first moments. Thank people for raising things early. Never ask 'who did this?' during an incident. Ask 'what do we know?' and 'what are we trying next?'. Save the learning for the postmortem, and keep that blameless too.

Safety isn't a poster on the wall. It's what happens to the first person who admits a mistake.
